Good questions

Frequently asked

What happens if it rains?

We shift our route and come as soon as the grass is dry, usually the next day. We'll text you so you're never left wondering.

How do I pay?

We take cash and Zelle. You can pay at the time of service, or we can set up monthly billing.

Do I need to be home?

Nope. As long as the gates are unlocked and pets are inside, we'll take care of everything and let you know when we're done.

Do you do snow removal?

Yes! Snow removal is new this winter. Single-car driveways start at $30 per visit, and bigger driveways are quoted by size. Send us your address and we'll get you on the list before the first snowfall.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. We earn your business every week. If you ever want to pause or stop, just let us know.
