Good questions

Frequently asked

Why not just leave the leaves?

A thick layer of leaves smothers the grass over the winter, inviting bare spots and disease in spring. Clearing them gives your lawn a head start.

What does a cleanup include?

We rake and blow the leaves out of the lawn and beds, then haul everything away — sticks and winter mess included.

When should I book?

Fall cleanup spots fill up fast once the leaves start falling. Booking early gets you on the route before the rush.

How do I pay?

We take cash and Zelle. You can pay at the time of service, or we can set up monthly billing.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. Book one cleanup or put us on your calendar every fall — either way, no contract.
